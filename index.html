About me

Welcome to my page! I am a PhD student in modelling. My research focuses on modelling competence and Pedagogical Content Knowledge for Scientific Modelling (PCK-SM) in pre-service and in-service teachers. I investigate how teachers acquire modelling competence and PCK-SM and design, test, and revise lessons around models and modelling across STEM disciplines. My main research question lies at the core of this: How can we support teachers to implement more authentic modelling practices in their classrooms?

With a background in educational science, I look at modelling in science education from a pedagogical perspective. I am interested in how to teach authentic scientific practice to foster scientific reasoning and critical thinking, as these are core components of modern citizenship. Due to my previous work at Utrecht's university of applied science, I also value the connection between research and practice. I therefore strive for productive collaboration with teachers, teacher educators, and curriculum/educational tool developers when doing research.
